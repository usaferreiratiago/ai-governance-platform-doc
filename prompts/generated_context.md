# Approved Semantic Model Context

## Purpose

This file is automatically generated from the Power BI semantic model and is used as the approved context for the LLM.

---

## Business Domain

Enterprise Finance and Sales Analytics

---

## Approved Measures

### Net Revenue

Revenue excluding taxes, discounts, and returns.

Synonyms: revenue, sales, turnover, net sales.

### Gross Margin

Gross profit divided by net revenue.

Synonyms: margin, gross profit margin.

### Growth %

Percentage growth compared with the same period in the previous year.

Synonyms: growth rate, year-over-year growth.

### Units Sold

Total quantity of products sold.
